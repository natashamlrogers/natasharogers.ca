Why owned events

Your room. Your guests. Your outcome.

An owned event is one your company hosts and controls end to end: the guest list, the agenda, the experience and the follow-up. A customer conference, an executive dinner, a leadership offsite, a launch party: when you own the event, you own the audience and the relationships it creates.

That control is also what makes owned events hard to get right. I bring the strategy and the operational muscle so your team can focus on the conversations that matter.

What you get when you hire me

  • A clear goal for every event (pipeline, renewals, retention or team alignment), agreed before we book a venue.
  • Guest-list strategy aligned with sales and customer success, so the right people are in the room.
  • Full logistics: venues, catering, travel, gifting, AV, run-of-show and day-of coordination.
  • Budget and vendor management that stretches every dollar further.
  • Follow-up plays and CRM tracking (HubSpot, Salesforce) so results show up in your numbers.

FAQ

Questions teams ask first

Who should I hire to plan a B2B customer conference or executive dinner?

Look for a strategist who has owned events end to end and can connect them to revenue. I have 15 years of B2B event experience, grew a customer conference's attendance 30% year over year on a lower budget, and influenced $1M+ in pipeline from a single event program. I work with B2B and SaaS companies across Canada and the US.

What is an owned event?

An owned event is one your company hosts and controls end to end: the guest list, the agenda, the experience and the follow-up. Customer conferences, executive dinners, customer advisory boards, team offsites, roadshows and launch events are all owned events. Unlike sponsoring a trade show, you own the audience, the data and the relationships.

Do you only work in Calgary?

No. I'm based in Calgary, Alberta and work remote-first with teams across Canada and the United States, travelling to run events onsite.

What size events do you plan?

Anything from a 12-seat executive dinner to a multi-day customer conference with hundreds of attendees, plus recurring programs like dinner series and webinar programs of 400+ events a year.

Can you work alongside our in-house marketing team?

Yes. Engagements range from a short strategy sprint, to full end-to-end production, to a fractional event lead who works inside your marketing, sales and customer success teams.

How far in advance should we reach out?

For a customer conference, six to nine months is ideal. Executive dinners and offsites can often be delivered in six to ten weeks.

How do you measure event ROI?

We agree on success metrics before planning starts, usually pipeline created or influenced, renewals and expansions, attendance and NPS. Then we track them in your CRM, such as HubSpot or Salesforce, with a post-event report.
